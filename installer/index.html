Alarm keypad
Firmware installer

A wall keypad for your Alarmo alarm.

Turns a Seeed SenseCAP Indicator D1 into a touch keypad for Alarmo in Home Assistant. Install it straight from this page.

Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Plug the Indicator in with a USB-C data cable first.

Step 1

Install

  1. Click Install and choose the Indicator's serial port.
  2. After flashing you can enter Wi-Fi here, or skip it and set Wi-Fi up on the keypad's screen.
Step 2

Set up

  1. On the keypad, connect to Wi-Fi (on screen, or with your phone via the QR code).
  2. In Home Assistant, add the discovered ESPHome device.
  3. In Alarmo, turn on MQTT. Then fill in the keypad's MQTT Broker, Username and Password under the device's Configuration in Home Assistant.

Full guide: github.com/DomMonte/sensecap-alarm-keypad
