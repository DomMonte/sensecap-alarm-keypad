SenseCAP Alarm Keypad

Turn a Seeed SenseCAP Indicator D1 into a wall keypad for Alarmo in Home Assistant.

  1. Plug the Indicator into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial is required).
  3. Click Install and choose the serial port.
  4. After flashing you can enter Wi-Fi here, or skip it and set up Wi-Fi on the keypad's screen.

Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Then, on the keypad:
  1. Connect to Wi-Fi (on screen, or with your phone via the QR code).
  2. Add the discovered ESPHome device in Home Assistant.
  3. Scan the QR code to import the Alarmo bridge blueprint and create an automation.

Full guide: github.com/DomMonte/sensecap-alarm-keypad
